📱 MOBILE CONTROLS
👆 Touch and drag on the table — move your racket around your side
⚡ POWER button — arm a power shot before the ball reaches your racket
↶ LEFT / ↷ RIGHT buttons — arm a left or right curve before the ball reaches your racket
🎯 Move the touch point left/right — aim the shot; move it forward/back — control the depth
💡 The action buttons appear only while the game is being played.
🎮 CONTROLS
🖱️ Move the trackpad/mouse — move the racket around your side of the table
🖱️ HOLD the trackpad down + move left/right — TURN the racket head
⌨️ Arrow keys — move the racket around your side
⚡ UP ARROW — charge a POWER SHOT when your racket hits the ball; the ball goes faster and glows on fire
⏱️ POWER SHOT cooldown — 20 seconds; the timer is shown at the top
🌀 LEFT / RIGHT ARROW while hitting — CURVE SHOT; the ball keeps bending through the air
⏱️ CURVE SHOT cooldown — 10 seconds after each curve shot
🎯 You can move across almost your entire side of the table
📖 RULES
• Every rally starts from a racket, never from the net
• The ball must clear the net and land on the table
• If the ball leaves either side, the opponent wins the point
• If the ball hits the net and fails to cross, the opponent wins the point
• Every rally scores a point — no second serves
• First player to reach 10 points wins the round
• Win a round to earn a 🏆 trophy and unlock the next arena
Choose Beginner, Easy, Medium, or Hard at any time. Higher levels make the CPU faster, more accurate, better at reading curve shots, and occasionally capable of returning a power shot.
